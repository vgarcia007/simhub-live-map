How does sync work?
Navi checks the Windows share every minute and keeps a local copy of new and changed SimHub recordings. Share the SimHub folder or its PluginsData folder with read access for your Windows user. Cached recordings and manual uploads are available in Recordings even with sync off or the Windows PC offline.
